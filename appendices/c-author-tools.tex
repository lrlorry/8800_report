\section{Research Roles and Inherited Work}
Rui Luo is the COMP8800 project presenter and thesis author;
Dr Mengxuan Zhang is the supervisor. The associated project manuscript
names both as coauthors \cite{adc_artifact}. This thesis expands the
correctness arguments, newer measurements, supplementary audits and
discussion of unsuccessful designs. The author list identifies joint
work; it does not assign every artifact to unaided individual work.

the representation foundation \cite{jhq}. IVF, product quantisation,

authorship. The project's GPU construction/execution system, workload-level

\cref{tab:1-contributions}. Source histories, run records and analysis
ledgers establish the provenance of the corresponding artifacts.

\section{Review, Traceability and Responsibility}
Review comments were checked against source and measurements.
Corrections include selector scope, budget-transfer conditions, OpenAI
TF32 arithmetic and construction-memory lifetimes. Retained notes
distinguish measurements, numerical identities, conditional proofs,
cost models and unperformed checks.

Bibliographic citations acknowledge inherited methods; artifact ledgers
identify code and measurements; review notes document assisted revisions.
These records describe the retained workflow, not a complete export of
every interaction or a signed personal attestation. Rui Luo remains
responsible for the submitted thesis, its use of sources and its
interpretation of adopted material.

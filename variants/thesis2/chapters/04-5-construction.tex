\subsection{Bounded input batches and two-pass construction}

Whole-database raw/rotated storage is $4Nd+4Nd=8Nd$ bytes. Bounded staging avoids it. For $n_b$ pipeline
buffers, configured add rows $a_{\rm add}$ and per-array byte budget
$S_{\rm stage}$, the source stages
\begin{equation}
 a_{\rm in}=\max\!\left\{1,\min\!\left(a_{\rm add},
 \left\lfloor\frac{S_{\rm stage}}{4d}\right\rfloor\right)\right\},
 \qquad S_{\rm float}=8n_ba_{\rm in}d.
 \label{eq:4-staging-rows}
\end{equation}
\[
 4d\leq S_{\rm stage},\quad n_b=3,\ S_{\rm stage}=128\,\mathrm{MiB}
 \Longrightarrow S_{\rm float}\leq2n_bS_{\rm stage}=768\,\mathrm{MiB}.
\]
Assignment/encoder scratch is additional.

\subsection{Phase occupancy and the process peak}

The one-pass estimate is
\begin{equation}
 H_{\rm single}=N(2b_r+3M+20)+2^{30}\quad\text{bytes}.
 \label{eq:4-peak-heuristic}
\end{equation}
Its ten live arrays sum to
\[
 3NM+2Nb_r+20N=N(3M+2b_r+20).
\]
The 1\,GiB allowance is heuristic, not a process-peak bound.

Final two-pass repack counts IDs, residuals, corrections and two primary copies:
\begin{equation}
 S_{\rm repack,2}=N(b_r+2M+8).
 \label{eq:4-two-pass-repack}
\end{equation}
 Whole-build peak also considers earlier pipeline/inverse scratch, model, sort, context and later workspace allocations.

can be missed.

Assignment-product capacity uses an independent configured row count
$a_{\rm assign}$, selected by \texttt{JHQ\_ASSIGN\_BATCH} or the source
default: 32,768 for at least 8,192 lists, otherwise 8,192.
\begin{equation}
 S_{\rm dots}=4n_b n_{\rm list}a_{\rm assign}\quad\text{bytes}.
 \label{eq:4-assignment-scratch}
\end{equation}
Allocation capacity is independent of active/staged rows. At $d=1536/3072$, defaults both allocate 3\,GiB. For BGE/Stella, $3\times4=12$\,GiB assignment scratch precedes gathering; \cref{fig:2-memory-lifetimes} separates those lifetimes.
\[
 S_{\rm peak}=\max_t\sum_{A\ {\rm live\ at}\ t}|A|.
\]
Stella's smaller repack plus model/context gives its lower observed peak.

For held-out recall/QPS $\widehat r,\widehat Q$ and $\widehat r^*=\max_{\alpha\in A}\widehat r(\alpha)$,
\begin{equation}
 \alpha_{\rm oracle}\in\operatorname*{arg\,max}_{\alpha\in A}
 \left\{\widehat Q(\alpha):
 \widehat r(\alpha)\geq\widehat r^*-\delta\right\},\qquad\delta=10^{-3}.
 \label{eq:6-oracle}
\end{equation}
This labelled/timed diagnostic uses recall slack $\delta$, not slot allowance $\epsilon$.

FULL tightens $\epsilon/(Sk)$ without labels; enumeration checks search correctness. Agreement minimises accepted budget, while the oracle maximises quality-constrained speed.

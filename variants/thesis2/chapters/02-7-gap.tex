controls specified in Chapter 3.

At nine bits per dimension, IVF-RaBitQ matches JHQ's nominal one-bit
primary plus eight-bit residual payload. The performance frontiers also
include four- and eight-bit IVF-RaBitQ; total memory use is assessed separately.

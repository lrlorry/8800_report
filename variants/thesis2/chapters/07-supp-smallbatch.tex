At batch 8/recall 0.97, CAGRA fp32 wins all four datasets under both estimators (JHQ ratios 0.641--0.893). At batch 32, ratios are 0.622--1.008; OpenAI-3072 favours JHQ under the measured threshold. Vogue's batch-8/0.95 lead requires interpolation. Grid resolution changes near ties.

Including qualifying endpoints, recall-0.90 ratios are 1.032--1.479 (batch 8), 0.866--1.166 (32), with three/one strict JHQ wins. Omitting endpoints overstates gains. Scheduling gains $1.7$--$3.3\times$ over one block at 0.95, without ensuring cross-system wins.

Null-call medians are 0.012--0.072\,ms and remain included. CAGRA uses Python, JHQ/RaBitQ C++; warm-ups differ (\cref{sec:a-t4-t5}).

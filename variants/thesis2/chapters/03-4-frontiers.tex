Dominance and the measured frontier are
\[
 a\succ b\iff R_a\geq R_b,\ Q_a\geq Q_b,\
 (R_a,Q_a)\ne(R_b,Q_b).
\]
\begin{equation}
Q_{\rm meas}(r)=\max\{Q_i:R_i\geq r\}.
\label{eq:3-measured-frontier}
\end{equation}
No qualifying measured point means undefined. For adjacent frontier points,
\begin{equation}
\log Q_{\rm int}(r)=(1-\lambda)\log Q_a+\lambda\log Q_b,
\qquad \lambda=\frac{r-R_a}{R_b-R_a}.
\label{eq:3-interpolated-frontier}
\end{equation}
Within $5\times10^{-4}$, the parser snaps; otherwise it interpolates without extrapolation. Qualifying endpoints remain eligible in T5. Near ties report both estimators; memory is unmatched.

Failed/invalid outputs are excluded; repeat-ID flags alone are not. Isolated reruns replace contaminated builds; skipped Stella batch-1,000 runs add no points (\cref{sec:a-e1-protocol}). Int8 remains included (\cref{fig:7-frontiers}); ADC/poster plots have separate scopes.

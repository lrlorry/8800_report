IVF routes to $n_{\rm probe}$ lists:
\[
 U(q)=\bigcup_{\ell\in\mathcal L(q)}\mathrm{list}_\ell,\qquad n_c=|U(q)|.
\]
A neighbour outside $U$ cannot be recovered by later scoring.

PQ learns codebooks by subspace \cite{pq}. With unquantised $q$, ADC uses
\begin{equation}
 \|q-\widehat x\|^2=\sum_{m=0}^{M-1}\|q_m-\mathcal C_m[z_m]\|^2.
 \label{eq:2-adc}
\end{equation}
Lookups replace coordinate scoring. A learned subcodebook need not itself be Cartesian; Chapter 5 relies on that extra structure.

The residual definitions differ:
\[
 r_{\rm IVF\text{-}PQ}=x-\mu_{\ell(x)},\qquad
 r_{\rm JHQ}=\Pi x-\widehat p(x).
\]
JHQ therefore adds no IVF centroid during reconstruction. Candidate expansion/refinement is established \cite{johnson_gpu,faiss_refine}.

With $|U(q)|\geq k$ and $c=\max\{k,\lceil\alpha k\rceil\}$,
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal R_\alpha(q)\subseteq C_\alpha(q)\subseteq U(q)\subseteq\mathcal X.
 \label{eq:2-cascade}
\end{equation}
For GT set $\mathcal T_k$, set inclusion gives
\[
 |\mathcal R_\alpha\cap\mathcal T_k|
 \leq |C_\alpha\cap\mathcal T_k|
 \leq |U\cap\mathcal T_k|.
\]
Loss attribution requires fixed-universe counterfactual scores.

Residual access is restricted to primary survivors, reducing coordinate
work from $O(n_cd)$ to $O(cd)$. Lane cooperation accelerates that work
at a fixed budget.

\subsection{Reconstructed scoring and the query-only constant}

With primary reconstruction $\widehat p$, residual $\widehat r$ and stored correction $\gamma=2\langle\widehat p,\widehat r\rangle$, refinement computes
\begin{align}
 \refineddist(q,x)
 &=\primarydist(q,x)+\|q'-\widehat r(x)\|_2^2+\gamma(x)
 \label{eq:5-refined-score}\\
 &=\|q'-\widehat p(x)-\widehat r(x)\|_2^2+\|q'\|_2^2.
 \label{eq:5-refined-constant}
\end{align}
Norm expansion gives the second equality. The query-only constant preserves ranking, while reconstruction error remains \cite{jhq}.

The scalar path sums coordinate-wise residual differences from $q'$, staging queries and, when possible, codebooks in shared memory. It then adds the primary score and correction.

\subsection{Mapping lanes to words and reducing partial sums}

At $\lrefine=L\in\{1,8,16,32\}$, adjacent lanes share one candidate. A residual word holds $d_{\rm word}=32/B_R$ codes: four for eight-bit, eight for four-bit. Lane $a$ reads words $a,a+L,\ldots$; butterfly shuffles sum partials and lane zero writes the score.

L0 uses scalar byte loads; L1 packs word loads but keeps one lane and the accumulation order. L8/L16/L32 divide coordinate work, shortening each walk at the cost of shuffles and fewer concurrent candidates. Larger $L$ need not win; survivor locality and codebook placement still matter.

\subsection{Final selection and the numerical boundary}

Given $k$ finite valid survivors with unique IDs, one block selects exact top-$k$ under $(\refineddist,\mathrm{ID})$. Each round scans all $c$ slots, emits the minimum and sets its score to infinity. There is no per-thread quota. If finite entries run out, a valid smallest ID can repeat with infinity; an entirely invalid pool returns invalid IDs. Evaluated pools satisfy the finite-entry contract.

Final ties use database IDs; primary ties use positions. Refinement cannot restore excluded candidates or remove quantisation error. Lane reductions change FP32 association and may move near ties.

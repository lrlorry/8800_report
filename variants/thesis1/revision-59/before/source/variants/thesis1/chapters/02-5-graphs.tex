SONG exposes distance parallelism in GPU graph search \cite{song}. CAGRA designs GPU construction and navigation, tuned by connectivity and search width \cite{cagra}. We test both fp32 and quantised-vector CAGRA because their speed and storage differ.

BANG pairs GPU compressed vectors with a host-resident graph \cite{bang}. This trades transfer costs for capacity. JHQ's index remains device-resident.

\subsection{Mailman-style shared sums: arithmetic reuse meets scatter cost}
\label{sec:8-mailman}

Mailman reuses finite-alphabet matrix--vector sums \cite{mailman}. We test atomic buckets, private histograms and widths 4/6/8 against packed two-table scoring on frozen Vogue/arXiv/OpenAI-3072 streams, 128 probes, batches 8/1,000. Codes and mathematical scores stay fixed.

The best alternatives reach only 0.187--0.581 of baseline throughput (\cref{fig:8-negative}). Profiles spend most cycles in bucket accumulation: list-local bias repeats destinations; private histograms trade atomics for larger state and shared updates. Narrower groups help some variants, but none wins. This stopped these implementations, not all finite-alphabet reuse.

The pilot emits scores, not neighbours. FP32/FP64 cutoff differences therefore do not establish pipeline equivalence. With scorer ratio $s$ and replaceable fraction $f$, $1/[(1-f)+f/s]$ is only a replacement model: integration stopped before end-to-end testing. A lossless-dictionary study also stopped at structural statistics. Neither supplies a measured system-frontier point.

\subsection{Rank-oriented coding: a local gain is not a general replacement}
\label{sec:8-rank}

The ranking study replays exported candidates in FP64 with 400/200/400 calibration/validation/test queries. It tests weighted scores, median/list thresholds, learned losses and linear-MMSE decoding. Better reconstruction or inner-product MSE can still worsen nearest-candidate order; changing primary levels also changes residual reference points.

On arXiv test queries, median thresholds cut the minimum tested qualifying survivor count from 875 to 375; the other two workloads fail the same strict criterion. Rotation preserves corpus-mean norm, so Gaussian intuition does not remove bias. Centring may help, but must also preserve the refined-score ceiling. The improvement is workload-specific.

A separate GPU follow-up imports median thresholds/levels and retrains residuals. At arXiv's original $\alpha=100$ target, gains are about 1.40/1.21 at batches 8/1,000. Vogue stops at recall 0.9630, below target 0.9644; OpenAI-3072 is tied. These three-round results use separate states/queries and are excluded from E1.

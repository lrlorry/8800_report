\subsection{Complete decision traces and finite-pool risk}
\label{sec:7-budget-trace}

All 2,304 sampled and twelve FULL curves are monotone and match enumeration. Recorded subset sums reproduce 18,432 grid counts (\cref{eq:6-finite-pool}). This checks those aggregates, not arbitrary subset/position invariance or the distance-only cases' general nesting (\cref{sec:6-3}).

Maximum sampled loss reaches 0.0050 (arXiv/512) and 0.0046 (BGE/512) at $S=128$, and 0.0294 at $S=32$. FULL stays within 0.0006.

ArXiv/128 predicted/observed crossing rates are 0.965/0.984, 0.875/0.875 and 0.620/0.625 for $S=32,64,128$ (\cref{fig:7-finite-pool}). Across 36 comparisons, the largest gap is BGE/512's 0.144 (0.519/0.375). Prediction assumes stable counts and uses held-out fixed-arm quality to map choices to GT loss; calibration remains label-free.

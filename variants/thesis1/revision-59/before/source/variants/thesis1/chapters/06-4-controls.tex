Let $\widehat r,\widehat Q$ denote held-out recall/QPS and $\widehat r^*=\max_{\alpha\in A}\widehat r(\alpha)$. The diagnostic oracle is
\begin{equation}
 \alpha_{\rm oracle}\in\operatorname*{arg\,max}_{\alpha\in A}
 \left\{\widehat Q(\alpha):
 \widehat r(\alpha)\geq\widehat r^*-\delta\right\},\qquad\delta=10^{-3}.
 \label{eq:6-oracle}
\end{equation}
It uses labels and timings. Its recall slack $\delta$ differs from agreement's slot allowance $\epsilon$.

FULL remains label-free bisection and tightens $\epsilon/(Sk)$. Same-sample enumeration checks the search procedure. Agreement minimises an accepted multiplier; the oracle maximises quality-constrained speed, so their choices can differ.

Fixed 64 tests a smaller default; fixed 200 tests a high budget (\cref{sec:7-5,tab:7-budget-quality}).

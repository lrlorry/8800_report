\subsection{Risk-controlled agreement with a reuse-aware objective}

A future policy could minimise calibration-plus-reuse time with valid query-level reference-agreement bounds. It needs matched-regime counts, a fixed reference, explicit sampling/order assumptions and drift-triggered renewal. An absolute GT target additionally needs labels or justified reference-to-GT assumptions.

Compare fixed sampling, reusable rank curves and cost-aware selection on held-out pools, measuring total time and tail agreement. An older four-cell sequential controller lost because extra launches outweighed sample savings. Success must therefore save total time against a quality-matched control while satisfying the stated risk bound.

\subsection{Capacity-aware residual placement}

A residual cache could keep primary codes on-device and fetch missed residual blocks from host memory under a fixed device cap. Hold codes, routing, survivors and arithmetic fixed; vary cap, batch and locality. Measure transfer bytes, hits, overlap, tail latency and throughput. It must extend usable capacity at acceptable cost. This placement study is unimplemented; lowering $\alpha$ does not provide it.

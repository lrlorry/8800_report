\Cref{tab:3-design} defines the comparison unit and what would challenge
each research claim. A \emph{cell} fixes a dataset, index configuration and
workload setting; arms are alternative implementations or budgets within
that cell. For paired execution measurements, the index state, queries,
probe count, refinement budget and scoring representation are held fixed
unless the named treatment changes them. The recorded paired ratio is
$Q_{\rm treatment}/Q_{\rm control}$. Geometric means summarise multiplicative
ratios over the stated cells, giving each cell equal weight.

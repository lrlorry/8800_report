Primary screening limits residual-coordinate work to $O(cd)$ instead of $O(n_cd)$. Lanes cooperate at the same budget.

\subsection{Reconstructed scoring and the query-only constant}

With $\gamma=2\langle\widehat p,\widehat r\rangle$, real-arithmetic expansion gives
\begin{align}
 \refineddist(q,x)
 &=\primarydist(q,x)+\|q'-\widehat r(x)\|_2^2+\gamma(x)
 \label{eq:5-refined-score}\\
 &=2\|q'\|_2^2+\|\widehat p+\widehat r\|_2^2-2\langle q',\widehat p+\widehat r\rangle\nonumber\\
 &=\|q'-\widehat p(x)-\widehat r(x)\|_2^2+\|q'\|_2^2.
 \label{eq:5-refined-constant}
\end{align}
The query-only constant preserves ranking; reconstruction error remains \cite{jhq}.

The scalar path sums squared coordinate differences between $q'$ and residual reconstruction, then adds the primary score/correction. Queries and fitting codebooks use shared memory.

\subsection{Mapping lanes to words and reducing partial sums}

For $\lrefine=L\in\{1,8,16,32\}$,
\[
 d_{\rm word}=32/B_R,\qquad
 \text{words of lane }a:\ a,a+L,a+2L,\ldots.
\]
A word holds four eight-bit or eight four-bit codes. Butterfly shuffles sum lane partials; lane zero writes the score.

\subsection{Final selection and the numerical boundary}

For at least $k$ finite valid survivors with unique IDs, each round computes
\[
 i^*=\arg\min_{i<c}(D_{H,i},\mathrm{ID}_i),\quad d^*=D_{H,i^*},\quad
 D_{H,i^*}\leftarrow+\infty
\]
using all slots and emits $(\mathrm{ID}_{i^*},d^*)$: exact retained-pool top-$k$, without thread quotas. After finite entries run out, a smallest valid ID may repeat at infinity; all-invalid pools return invalid IDs. Evaluated pools meet the finite-entry contract.

Final ties use database IDs; primary ties use positions. Refinement cannot recover discarded candidates or quantisation loss. Lane FP32 association can move near ties.

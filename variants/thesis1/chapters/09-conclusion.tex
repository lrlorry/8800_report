\section{Answers to the Four Research Questions}
\label{sec:9-1}
\paragraph{RQ1: Construction and capacity.}
GPU training, batched encoding, IVF placement and packing build the resident hierarchy. The largest test holds 17.78 million vectors at 20.44\,GiB, built in 10.54--10.65\,s (\cref{sec:7-7}). Sampled peaks are Stella 22.58\,GiB and BGE 26.08\,GiB at add entry.

\paragraph{RQ2: Correct and efficient execution.}
Cartesian LUT splitting is exact in real arithmetic; complete-key retention/merging and witnessed bounds preserve grouped selection under the stated conditions. Two-group tables lead in 22/24 cells. Against lane refinement, scheduling gives geometric-mean $2.727\times$ at batch 8 and $1.015\times$ at 1,000: benefits depend on batch/work distribution (\cref{sec:7-3,sec:7-6}). T4's strict CPU
reference matches all 55,296 grouped instances in complete-key order;
56 one-block set differences are exclusively cutoff ties
(\cref{sec:7-strict-selection}).

\paragraph{RQ3: Refinement budget and quality.}
FULL calibration meets the tested relative
recall-loss target in 12/12 cells, with maximum observed loss 0.0006.
At $S=128$ reaches 40/64 crossings in one cell (\cref{sec:7-5}). All FULL choices match enumeration, but ten distance-only cells lack a general nesting guarantee. Reference agreement is not population GT recall. Four direct reuse tests show workload-dependent quality/time gains.

\paragraph{RQ4: Competitive operating regions.}
At batch 1,000/recall 0.97, JHQ leads on three datasets and nearly ties RaBitQ on arXiv; Vogue's lead needs grouping (\cref{sec:7-2}). OpenAI E1 uses TF32. Cached-model FP32 checks retain advantages over archived D baselines and contemporaneous T1 anchors, without FP32 retraining (\cref{sec:b-precision-anchors}). Int8 wins lower targets; RaBitQ wins some higher ones. CAGRA fp32 wins all batch-8 datasets; batch-32 near ties depend on the estimator (\cref{sec:7-smallbatch}).

\section{Closing Principle and Outlook}
\label{sec:9-2}
Representation, execution and effort must share a quality target. Next steps are reusable risk-controlled calibration and capacity-aware residual placement (\cref{sec:8-4}).

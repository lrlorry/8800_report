\subsection{Mailman-style shared sums: arithmetic reuse meets scatter cost}
\label{sec:8-mailman}

Mailman reuses finite-alphabet matrix--vector sums \cite{mailman}. We test atomic buckets, private histograms and widths 4/6/8 against packed two-table scoring on frozen Vogue/arXiv/OpenAI-3072 streams, 128 probes, batches 8/1,000. Codes and mathematical scores stay fixed.

The best alternatives reach only 0.187--0.581 of baseline throughput (\cref{fig:8-negative}). Bucket accumulation dominates profiles. List bias repeats destinations; private histograms need more state/shared updates. Narrower groups sometimes help, but no tested variant wins. We stopped these implementations, leaving other reuse designs open.

The pilot emits scores, so FP32/FP64 cutoff differences do not establish neighbour-pipeline equivalence. For scorer ratio $s$ and replaceable fraction $f$,
\[
 T'/T=(1-f)+f/s.
\]
This is a replacement model, not an end-to-end measurement: integration stopped. The dictionary study stopped at structural statistics; neither contributes a system-frontier point.

\subsection{Rank-oriented coding: a local gain is not a general replacement}
\label{sec:8-rank}

The ranking replay uses FP64 exported candidates and 400/200/400 calibration/\allowbreak{}validation/\allowbreak{}test queries. It tests weighted scores, median/list thresholds, learned losses and linear-MMSE decoding. Lower reconstruction/inner-product MSE can worsen neighbour order; changed primary levels also move residual reference points.

ArXiv median thresholds reduce the minimum tested qualifying count from 875 to 375; two other workloads fail the criterion. Rotation preserves corpus-mean norm, leaving bias despite Gaussian intuition. Centring must also preserve the refined-score ceiling; this gain is workload-specific.

A GPU follow-up imports median thresholds/levels and retrains residuals. At arXiv's original $\alpha=100$ target it gains $1.40/1.21\times$ at batches 8/1,000. Vogue reaches only 0.9630 versus target 0.9644; OpenAI-3072 ties. Three-round results use separate states/queries outside E1.

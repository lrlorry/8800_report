For OpenAI/BGE/Stella, preparation filters non-finite rows and applies
\[
 x\leftarrow x/\|x\|\quad(\|x\|>10^{-12}).
\]
Seed 42 samples queries without replacement. Stella uses 999 corpus queries, not its 65-query benchmark. Relevance labels are absent. The OpenAI exports share the sampling rule; checks support row alignment on all queries and 10,299 base rows, without original-ID joins (\cref{sec:7-precision,sec:7-2}).

All methods in a cell read the same query and GT files. Ground truth is
computed by exhaustive squared-L2 search over exported FP32 vectors; the
streamed converter maintains top-$k$ across database tiles. Evaluation uses
stored GT order, including its floating-point tie choices. With
$\mathcal T_k(q)$ the first $k$ GT IDs and $\mathcal R_k(q)$ the returned
ID set,
\begin{equation}
\Recall@k=\frac{1}{|\mathcal Q|k}
\sum_{q\in\mathcal Q}|\mathcal R_k(q)\cap\mathcal T_k(q)|.
\label{eq:3-recall}
\end{equation}
GT rows are 100 wide for Vogue/arXiv and 20 wide for the other four;
only ten entries are scored. Duplicate returned IDs do not earn repeated
credit. Range, duplicate and repeat-call checks are recorded separately.

JHQ uses $M=d/8$, $B=B_r=8$, eight coarse iterations and 100,000 residual-training rows. Sweeps reload models and re-encode rows; cold builds refit. Training uses database rows. \[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal Q_{\rm cal}&=\{q_i:i\bmod2=0\},&
 \mathcal Q_{\rm hold}&=\{q_i:i\bmod2=1\},\\
 (|\mathcal Q_{\rm cal}|,|\mathcal Q_{\rm hold}|)
 &=\begin{cases}(500,499),&\text{Stella},\\(500,500),&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}
 \end{aligned}
 \]
Seeded subsamples of sizes
32, 64 and 128 are drawn 64 times per cell; FULL uses all 500 calibration
queries. Labels assess the oracle and holdout; agreement uses outputs (\cref{sec:6-2,sec:a-budget-contract}).

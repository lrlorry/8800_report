\frontchapter{Research Provenance and Tool Disclosure}
\label{front:provenance}
This thesis presents the JHQ-GPU project undertaken by \thesisauthor{} for
\thesiscourse{} under the supervision of \thesissupervisor{}.

The underlying JHQ representation and primary/residual hierarchy are
attributed to Jiabao Han, Mengxuan Zhang and Goce Trajcevski \cite{jhq}.
IVF, product quantisation, existing GPU selection/merge techniques and
baseline software retain their cited authorship. Rui Luo and Mengxuan
Zhang are coauthors of the associated project manuscript
\cite{adc_artifact}; their author order does not establish individual
task allocation. The thesis expands the correctness arguments,
measurements, supplementary checks and discussion of unsuccessful designs.
\Cref{sec:1-5,tab:1-contributions} identify the project's relationship
to that manuscript and map its technical contributions.

\paragraph{Generative-AI assistance.}
Project records document the following uses, identified by product name
without inferring model versions:
\textbf{OpenAI Codex} assisted with English/Chinese writing and revision,
LaTeX, diagrams, analysis scripts, source/log inspection, offline numerical
checks and PDF compilation \cite{ai_codex_record}.
\textbf{ChatGPT} supplied frameworks and chapter reviews concerning evidence,
scope and experimental design \cite{ai_chatgpt_record}.
\textbf{Anthropic Claude} assisted with implementation, experiment
automation and review; supplementary records cover construction,
representation/precision checks, budget traces, strict selection and
small-batch baselines \cite{ai_claude_record}. Selected original Codex
prompts and their artifact mapping are retained in
\href{https://github.com/lrlorry/8800_report/blob/main/docs/ai-use-record.md}{\nolinkurl{docs/ai-use-record.md}}.
This is a partial record: complete original ChatGPT/Claude conversations,
earlier interaction timestamps and model versions are not archived there.
The joint manuscript separately discloses
Claude/ChatGPT assistance with implementation, harnesses, figures and
writing, retaining that manuscript's versioned scope.

\paragraph{Traceability and responsibility.}
Source histories, run records and analysis ledgers establish artifact
provenance. \path{scripts/} retains analysis code;
\path{assessment/} records accepted and corrected review recommendations;
the code repository's \path{experiments/thesis_evidence/} retains
supplementary experiment records. Appendices~\ref{app:reproducibility}
and \ref{app:supplementary} identify the experimental contracts and
supporting evidence. Bibliographic citations acknowledge inherited
methods. These records describe the retained workflow, rather than a
complete interaction export or a signed personal attestation.
Responsibility for the submitted text, attribution and interpretation
remains with the thesis author.

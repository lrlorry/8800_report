JHQ-GPU merits evaluation when a compressed resident hierarchy fits the
device, batches provide useful parallel work, and the required recall lies
within its measured competitive region. The recorded FULL policy is the
quality-first calibration starting point, followed by validation on the
deployment's own queries and direct reuse timing. At lower recall, int8
CAGRA is a strong alternative; at some higher targets, IVF-RaBitQ wins.
Applications requiring small-request latency, updates or a population recall
guarantee need additional evidence. The deployment choice should compare
quality, total time and memory together under the actual service workload.

JHQ-GPU merits evaluation when a compressed resident hierarchy fits the
device and the required recall lies within its measured competitive region.

measured threshold; the other three favour CAGRA. Lower-recall JHQ
advantages also depend on dataset and grid. Grouped scheduling helps
JHQ internally without guaranteeing a cross-system lead
(\cref{sec:7-smallbatch}).

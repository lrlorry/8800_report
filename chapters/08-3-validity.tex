Internal validity is strongest for same-process paired interventions and
weakest where versions, trained states or interfaces change together.
\Cref{tab:8-threats} identifies the possible reversal for each main
interpretation. The main safeguards are the shared inputs, explicit score
contracts, retained timing arrays and versioned ledgers of Chapter 3.
Repeated calls estimate conditional timing variation; they do not estimate
the distribution over independently retrained indexes. Five supplementary builds now quantify recall variation at fixed operating
points, not the distribution of entire retrained frontiers. Precision
sensitivity also changes add/query arithmetic under a common cached model.

External validity is limited by one GPU, squared-L2 top-10 search, six
prepared embedding datasets and E1's full-batch comparison. Query removal
by position does not certify the absence of near-duplicate embeddings.
The exports are not a relevance-labelled retrieval benchmark. Database
updates, concurrent traffic, deployment latency objectives and changing
query populations are untested. The index is resident in compressed form;
the result supplies no billion-scale or multi-device capacity evidence.
These limitations identify the experiments needed before transferring an
observed operating region to a deployment.

Exactness claims name the computed score and ordering assumptions;
raw-vector recall also depends on routing and reconstruction. Independent
references have finite coverage, paired timings retain their workload
controls, and byte/payback models retain their accounting assumptions.
Population and ordering conditions are analysed in
\cref{sec:6-2,sec:6-3}; \cref{sec:8-3} consolidates the limits arising
from shared indexes, finite pools, tuning grids, interfaces and one device.

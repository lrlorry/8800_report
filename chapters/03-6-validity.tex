Proofs establish properties under their stated assumptions; independent
references test implementation agreement on finite cases. Paired benchmarks
measure an intervention within a fixed workload, and system measurements
locate operating regions on the tested device and grids. Byte and payback
models retain their accounting assumptions; proposed explanations require
separate tests. Exact primary selection concerns the computed primary score,
whereas raw-vector recall also depends on routing and reconstruction.


Shared-index dependence, finite pools, grid selection, interface
asymmetry and one-device coverage motivate the controls above.
The population and ordering assumptions are analysed in
\cref{sec:6-2,sec:6-3}; \cref{sec:8-3} consolidates the resulting limits
to interpretation and deployment.

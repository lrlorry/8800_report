The evidence ledger \path{data/ch3/audit.json} identifies configurations,
measurement boundaries and analysis inputs. Family-specific versions and
controls remain separate. Hashes identify consumed files; historical
executable and trained-state identities are incomplete. Appendix A lists
available reproduction inputs, and the repository supplement gives the
registry fields.\footnote{\path{docs/supplementary-evidence.md}.}

\path{scripts/build_ch7.py} regenerates the principal plots and tables,
including \cref{fig:7-frontiers,fig:7-lut,fig:7-memory};
\path{scripts/audit_ch3.py} checks the common protocol.
These are offline reconstructions. Repeating GPU measurements additionally
requires the listed binaries, datasets and trained states.

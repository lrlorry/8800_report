\chapter{System Architecture and GPU Index Construction}
\label{ch:architecture}
This chapter addresses RQ1: how to construct a searchable, GPU-resident
JHQ index from high-dimensional vectors. JHQ supplies the transform and
primary/residual representation \cite{jhq}. The systems problem is to
train that representation, encode a database larger than its available
full-precision device storage, and expose consistent list-ordered arrays
to the query path. The discussion follows training, placement, allocation and validation. Detailed query
kernels follow in \Cref{ch:gpu-execution}; measured comparisons are in
\Cref{sec:7-7}.

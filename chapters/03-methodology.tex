\chaptermark{Research Design and Measurement}
The evaluation connects a systems intervention to an observable cost or
quality change. It combines paired execution ablations, held-out budget
selection and independently tuned system frontiers. These comparisons answer
different questions: a faster kernel, a useful calibration policy and a
competitive search system require different controls. This chapter defines
those controls and the evidence needed to interpret them. Chapters 4--6
describe the mechanisms; Chapter 7 presents their measured outcomes.

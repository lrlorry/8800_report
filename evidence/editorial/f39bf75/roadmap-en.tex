\section{Thesis Roadmap}
\label{sec:1-6}
Chapters 2--3 establish the background and measurement design.
Chapters 4--6 develop index construction, GPU execution and workload-level
calibration. Chapter 7 evaluates their correctness, quality, cost and
competitive regions. Chapter 8 discusses negative results, limitations
and testable extensions; Chapter 9 answers the research questions.
Appendices A--B record reproduction contracts and supplementary evidence.

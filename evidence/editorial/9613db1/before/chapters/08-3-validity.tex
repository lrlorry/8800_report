External validity is limited by one GPU, squared-L2 top-10 and six
prepared embedding datasets. The batch-dependent operating regions are
reported in \cref{sec:7-smallbatch}. Query removal by position does not
certify the absence of near-duplicate embeddings, and the exports are not
a relevance-labelled retrieval benchmark. Database updates, concurrent
traffic, deployment latency objectives and changing query populations
remain untested. The resident-index results supply no billion-scale or
multi-device capacity evidence. These are the remaining checks before
transferring an observed region to deployment.
